City teams prepare for warmer summers

By Rowan Ellis · Published Tuesday morning

Public works teams in several districts are reviewing how streets and gathering places respond to longer periods of heat. The first round focuses on shade, drinking water, and access to indoor cooling sites.

The review began with resident reports and maintenance records. Teams are marking locations where trees are young, fountains need repair, or public buildings have limited evening hours.

Local observations guide the work

Neighborhood groups have shared notes from bus stops, school routes, and market streets. Those observations help staff compare a citywide map with conditions that change from block to block.

Officials said the current plan is a working document. Proposed changes will be reviewed with residents before construction schedules are set.

What happens next
